About

People-centred leadership, grounded in delivery.

I’m a Software Engineering Manager with a background in software and data engineering, architecture and analytics. Earlier work across utilities, financial services and the charity sector gives me a broader understanding of customer operations, commercial priorities and how organisations make decisions.

At Flutter Entertainment, I lead engineering delivery for customer-facing Free-to-Play products across multiple brands. I set direction and align priorities across engineering, product and delivery, while developing senior engineers with their own people-management responsibilities. My focus is on building leaders who can own their teams and delivery, supported by clear expectations, trust and accountability.

I build trust, psychological safety and clear accountability. I stay close enough to the technology to understand trade-offs and challenge decisions, while supporting engineers to own the implementation. My aim is to help teams deliver today and become more capable and self-sufficient over time.

Selected impact

How leadership turns into outcomes.

01

Developing people and leaders

Build leadership capability through coaching, trust and meaningful responsibility. Help emerging people managers develop their judgement and confidence, so they can support their teams and take ownership of delivery.

02

Leading complex change

Bring clarity when priorities, ownership or technology are changing. Set direction, make dependencies and trade-offs visible, and help teams move forward with a shared understanding of what matters.

03

Dependable customer delivery

Connect engineering priorities with customer needs and business commitments. Create the conditions for teams to deliver confidently, with quality, resilience and operational readiness considered throughout.

04

Leadership beyond one team

Work across engineering, product, delivery, architecture and wider business areas to resolve shared challenges and align priorities. Bring people together around decisions that improve delivery and build capability beyond the immediate team.

05

Putting AI into practice

Encourage engineers to explore where AI can make a useful difference, connect complementary ideas and help successful approaches reach other teams. Build confidence in adoption while keeping human judgement and accountability central.

06

Leading through incidents

Provide calm coordination when services are disrupted. Bring the right people together, establish clear priorities and maintain focus on restoring service, while supporting learning and improvements afterwards.
